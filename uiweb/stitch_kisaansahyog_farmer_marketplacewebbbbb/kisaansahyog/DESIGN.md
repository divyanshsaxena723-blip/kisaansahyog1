## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an accessible, high-trust digital environment built to connect farmers directly with buyers, institutions, and agricultural welfare services. The visual expression emphasizes dignity, directness, and effortless comprehension under varying outdoor light conditions and accessibility requirements.

### Design Movement & Core Philosophy
The interface operates under a refined modern agricultural aesthetic—grounded in calm utilitarianism, gentle rounded geometry, and generous breathing room. It strips away commercial clutter and predatory marketplace noise to present transparent prices, voice-first actions, and clear welfare updates. 

- **Approachable Utility:** Broad, open surfaces with high-clarity typography eliminate visual tension and digital friction for first-generation smartphone users.
- **Organic Reliability:** Natural hues derived from healthy leaf tissue, rich soils, and clear skies reassure producers that their transactions are secure, verified, and direct.
- **Sunlight-Ready Contrast:** Strict color ratios prioritize readability in bright sunlight and dusty environments without resorting to harsh visual strain.

## Colors

The palette uses dependable agricultural greens balanced by soft tonal surfaces, removing stark sterile grays in favor of warm, earthy neutral tones.

### Functional Color Applications
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFFFFF` serves as the primary canvas for maximum outdoor legibility and crisp separation.
- **Surface Tones:**
  - *Soft Mint (`#E8F5E9`):* Applied to primary card modules, state subsidies, and positive yield cards.
  - *Pastel Sky (`#E3F2FD`):* Used for weather alerts, logistics tracking, and cold-storage updates.
  - *Warm Cream (`#FFFDF5`):* Delicately distinguishes direct marketplace offers, Mandi price indices, and tender notices.
- **Interactive Action Colors:**
  - *Primary Seed Green (`#2E7D32`):* Used for major transactional triggers, primary CTAs, active sidebar states, and finalized bid approvals.
  - *Sage Accent (`#66BB6A`):* Active toggle states, progress indicators, and voice input waveforms.
- **Typography & Structural Contrast:**
  - *Deep Forest Grey (`#2F3E34`):* High-legibility text for headlines, price tags, and primary metric labels (WCAG AAA compliant against `#FFFFFF` and tonal card fills).
  - *Muted Slate Green (`#6B7F72`):* Subtitles, secondary metadata, unit labels (e.g., `/Quintal`), and subtle outline strokes.
- **State & System Alerts:**
  - *Soft Peach (`#FFE0B2`):* Pending escrows, dynamic price negotiations, and advisory warnings.
  - *Blush Red (`#FFCDD2`):* Urgent harvest alerts, weather warnings, and direct trade cancellations.

## Typography

The type system blends structural clarity with welcoming terminal shapes.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings & Labels):** Provides an optimistic, contemporary presence for key figures, metrics, rates, and titles. Its wide aperture ensures numbers and symbols remain instantly distinct even when zoomed or scaled on smaller screens.
- **Nunito Sans (Body & Continuous Reading):** The gently rounded letterforms provide supreme comfort during extended reading of government scheme guidelines, crop health advisories, and contract breakdowns.
- **Numerical Hierarchy:** All financial valuations and quantity tallies must render using `font-weight: 700` with tabular number spacing (`tnum`) enabled to prevent horizontal shifting during live market updates.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column layout organizes marketplace transactions, crop inventories, and welfare analytics into predictable, easy-to-scan visual patterns.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and a fixed `260px` vertical sidebar navigation pane. Outer canvas margin is set to `2rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, gutters contract to `1.25rem`, side navigation condenses into an icon-rail (`72px`), and top action bars reflow to 2 vertical tiers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` outer canvas padding. The persistent sidebar transitions to a bottom bar supplemented by a simplified persistent voice-and-search header.

### Spacing Principles
- Generous padding inside cards (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) prevents density fatigue.
- Direct marketplace listing grids must maintain equal horizontal and vertical gap spacing (`space-lg`) to preserve visual rhythm across crop catalog cards.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, dark silhouettes, and complex blur overlays, prioritizing luminous, air-filled dimension through subtle outlines and delicate environmental lift.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Native card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`, `#E8F5E9`, `#FFFDF5`, `#E3F2FD`) sit level on the base canvas framed by subtle borders: `1px solid rgba(107, 127, 114, 0.15)`.
- **Level 1 (Interactive Surface):** Applied to resting interactive tiles, active crop cards, and voice search bars:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(47, 62, 52, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(47, 62, 52, 0.02);`
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Drag):** Cards elevated on hover, dropdown menus, and voice recognition dialogs:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(47, 62, 52, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(47, 62, 52, 0.03);`
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Drawers):** Modals, full contract summaries, and bottom sheets:
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(47, 62, 52, 0.14);` accompanied by a backdrop overlay of `rgba(47, 62, 52, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a reassuring geometry with rounded contours (`roundedness: 2`). This approachable silhouette removes harsh corners, reinforcing care and community.

### Shape Tokens
- **Base Components (`rounded-md` / `0.5rem`):** Checkboxes, form fields, small tags, tooltips.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`):** Content cards, marketplace items, banner alerts, top action bars.
- **Feature Modules (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem`):** Main crop listings, weather advisory blocks, modal views, quick-action drawers.
- **Interactive Controls (`rounded-full`):** Filter pills, status badges, voice search triggers, action avatars, and audio recording buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid background of `#2E7D32`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded to `1rem` or full pill, `height: 48px` minimum for effortless tap targets. Never uses drop shadows that clash with the background.
- **Secondary Tone:** Solid background of `#E8F5E9`, text `#2E7D32`, no border.
- **Outlined / Tertiary:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border with `rgba(107, 127, 114, 0.3)`, text `#2F3E34`.

### Top Navigation & Global Header
- **Persistent Top Bar:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, bottom hairline border `1px solid rgba(107, 127, 114, 0.12)`.
- **Integrated Voice Search:** Central search input featuring a dedicated microphone icon in `#2E7D32` with a soft mint hover halo.
- **AI Shayog Assistant Pill:** A soft sky-blue (`#E3F2FD`) shortcut with a subtle spark icon, allowing instantaneous vernacular voice assistance.
- **Language Switcher:** Compact rounded pill toggle showing the current regional script (e.g., "हिन्दी / EN") for quick switching.
- **Notification Hub:** Soft rounded bell button paired with a `#FFCDD2` unread alert indicator.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** White fill, `1.5px` border of `rgba(107, 127, 114, 0.25)`, minimum height `48px`, font size `16px` to prevent automatic zoom on mobile devices.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#2E7D32` with a `3px` outer ring in `rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.15)`.
- **Selection Controls:** Checkboxes and radio buttons sized at `20px x 20px`, active states rendered in `#2E7D32`.

### Marketplace & Scheme Cards
- **Crop Cards:** Surfaces of pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or cream (`#FFFDF5`), framed by gentle 16px radii. Prominent crop image preview top-mounted, price per quintal highlighted in `Plus Jakarta Sans` `24px` bold in `#2F3E34`, and direct "Make Offer / Buy" button.
- **Welfare / Scheme Cards:** Mint tinted (`#E8F5E9`), featuring clear eligibility badges and direct vernacular audio summary icons.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Trade Active:** Soft mint background (`#E8F5E9`) with `#2E7D32` text.
- **Verification Pending:** Soft peach background (`#FFE0B2`) with `#A05A00` text.
- **Expiring / Hazard:** Blush red background (`#FFCDD2`) with `#B71C1C` text.
- **Geometry:** Height `28px`, padding `0 12px`, border-radius `9999px`, font `label-sm`.
